## Lore 🚙🚙🚙

Born in 2026, the Epitech Grand Prix incarnate its great philosophy : Crash & Learn.\
But here, you will safely crash more than a million times in our great racing simulation.

In this project, you will learn to use Reinforcement Learning, an AI learning method, to train your own driver.
And we all remember what Dominic Toretto said : *"it's not only about the reward function, it's about the family."*\
Hopefully each driver in your family may make you stronger.\
Because, the circuit is not only yours. It never was.

Instructions for the coming races and challenges will come in time.\
For now just pass your RL driving licence to complete the circuit alone #Gaijin.\
But one reminder, here we don't care about safety, what matters is your time and ranking among your peers.
